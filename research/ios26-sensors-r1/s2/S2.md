**By design, a mode-2 gate stop panics the boot** (`blazer_sensors.c`: `if (!boot_keep) panic("end of probe…")`); only a gate pass
into the stream returns quietly. PLAN.md's "a gate stop, no panic" was wrong about that. On the phone the gate passed in
sensor-stream-r2 (PHYSICAL, AoC not started), and nothing on the int1 line starts AoC. But sensor-stream-r2 ran **without**
`blazer_keys`/`blazer_haptic_tap`/netstack (its line: `blazer_sensors=2` on the p26 line), so the combination is new (INFERRED risk:
`the CPM owner refused a PMIC frame…`, a rail or pad stop). If the gate stops on the phone, the boot ends ~20-40 s after the kernel
starts with the panic text naming the stop, read from the ramdump. `blazer_sensors_keep=1` (22 B) would contain that but needs
another 21 B off the line and an allow-list entry; not done here.

## 5. Attended test (owner; standalone `fastboot boot`, then the return check)

```
tools/bootios research/images/a7-ios-system-sens2/boot.img a7-ios-system-sens2 \
  --mach research/ios26-sensors-r1/s2/build/kernel.sens2.unstripped --wait 1080
```

Expect int1's behaviour (lock screen, swipe up to Home, Settings/Phone). The boot ends itself at 900 s.

**No on-screen rotation is expected.** Home holds only Settings and Phone (INT1.md §2: other stock apps need root r4), and
Settings, Phone and the iPhone12,3 Home screen are portrait-only. So the test is log evidence. Steps after Home is up (~2 min):

1. Phone **flat on the table, screen up**, 10 s.
2. **Hold upright** (portrait), 10 s.
3. **Landscape, top to the left**, 10 s; then **top to the right**, 10 s. Back upright.
4. Flat again, then **turn it flat through N, E, S, W** (a slow quarter turn each, 10 s at each), away from laptops/magnets.
5. Then int1's quick checks if wanted (touch, a volume press): sensors must not cost touch/keys/Home.

Stop and put the phone down on: a panic screen/reset early in the boot (read the panic text from the ramdump), loss of Home or touch.

**Not a boot failure**: `compass not started: bus "…" detail …, mag "…" detail …` (report the detail; the IMU must keep streaming)
